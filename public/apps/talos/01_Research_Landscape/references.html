How the research was compiled

Method
Five research notes were written on 21 October 2026 from open web sources: company press releases and SEC filings, trade press (SpaceNews, Via Satellite, Breaking Defense, Payload, Military & Aerospace Electronics), mission databases (eoPortal, Gunter's Space Page), manufacturer datasheets and distributor pages (satsearch, SatNow, everythingRF), the NASA State-of-the-Art of Small Spacecraft Technology 2025/2026 editions, agency documents (EUSPA, EDA, ESA, NGA, NCIA, UNOOSA, ITU), analyst reports (IISS, Dataintelo, MarketsandMarkets) and a small number of blogs and newsletters where no better source existed. All links were live when checked.
Labelling
Figures are company or press claims unless stated otherwise. The notes use three flags that are carried onto every page of this site: claimed (a vendor or operator statement without independent confirmation, e.g. geolocation accuracies), unverified (a figure from secondary sources or community knowledge, e.g. most prices and the Kleos lifetime capital), and est. / [est.] (the study's own inference). An estimate is never upgraded to a fact between the research note and the page.
Numbering
Each note numbers its sources [n] inline; the site id is R<note>-<n>. Research note 02 lists its sources without numbers, so its entries carry synthetic ids R2-x62 onward. Two R1 numbers (3 and the duplicate of 2) refer to the same document and are merged under R1-2.
Baseline data
Numbers used in calculations live in assets/data/*.js (baseline, components, emitters, risks, programmatics, suppliers, operators table) and are rendered from there, so a correction in the data file propagates to every page.
